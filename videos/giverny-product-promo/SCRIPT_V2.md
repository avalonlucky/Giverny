# Giverny 品牌产品片 V2 · 65 秒脚本

## 中文主版

### 00:00–00:08 · 看不见的投入

需求散在聊天里，工时记在表格里，文件躺在网盘里。到了月底，创作者的真实投入，反而最难说清。

**屏幕短句：** 真实投入，不该消失。

### 00:08–00:15 · 为什么是 Giverny

Giverny 因此而生。它致敬莫奈的吉维尼：产品不只解决工作，也应该让创作保留生长的乐趣。

**屏幕短句：** 可靠的工具，也可以保留艺术。

### 00:15–00:26 · 工作开始留下证据

从一个需求开始，任务、排期、进展、实际工时和文件，都沉淀在同一条时间线上。

**屏幕短句：** 需求 → 进展 → 实际工时 → 文件

### 00:26–00:35 · 验收是边界

交付时，再核对进度、工时、附件和验收依据。确认之后，投入才成为可以结算的成果。

**屏幕短句：** 验收，是进入结算的边界。

### 00:35–00:45 · 对账不再重新证明

月底无需再翻聊天、找文件、手工对账。任务、工时、金额和交付件自动生成回单，也能安全分享给合作伙伴。

**屏幕短句：** 做了什么 · 花了多久 · 交付了什么 · 为什么结算

### 00:45–00:55 · Agent 在真实上下文里工作

爱丽丝理解任务与文件，发现风险，规划下一步；写入前先确认，结果可以验真。

**屏幕短句：** 理解上下文 · 写入前确认 · 结果可验真

### 00:55–01:05 · 品牌收束

让每一份投入被看见，让每一次创作留下风景。Giverny，让创作在自己的花园里生长。

**屏幕短句：** 让创作在自己的花园里生长。

## English edition

### 00:00–00:08 · Invisible work

Briefs live in chats. Hours live in spreadsheets. Files disappear into drives. By month-end, the creator's real contribution is often the hardest thing to explain.

**On screen:** Real work shouldn't disappear.

### 00:08–00:15 · Why Giverny

That's why Giverny exists. Inspired by Monet's garden, it believes a product should organize work without taking the life out of creation.

**On screen:** Reliable tools can still feel human.

### 00:15–00:26 · Work becomes evidence

From the first brief, tasks, schedules, progress, actual hours, and files stay together on one traceable timeline.

**On screen:** Brief → Progress → Actual time → Files

### 00:26–00:35 · Acceptance is the boundary

At delivery, progress, time, attachments, and approval evidence are checked once more. Only then does effort become a settlement-ready result.

**On screen:** Acceptance turns work into accountable results.

### 00:35–00:45 · No more reconstructing the month

No more searching chats, hunting files, or rebuilding the month by hand. Tasks, hours, amounts, and deliverables become a receipt you can safely share.

**On screen:** What was done · How long it took · What was delivered · Why it is billed

### 00:45–00:55 · An Agent inside the work

Alice understands tasks and files, spots risks, and plans what comes next. She asks before writing, and her results can be verified.

**On screen:** Context-aware · Confirmed actions · Verifiable results

### 00:55–01:05 · Brand resolution

Make every contribution visible, and let every creation leave a landscape behind. Giverny. Let creation grow in its own garden.

**On screen:** Let creation grow in its own garden.

## 表达要求

- 中文旁白温和、明确、有停顿，不朗诵诗歌，也不使用广告腔。
- “工时、金额、验收、结算”必须直接说清，不能全部改写成花园隐喻。
- “爱丽丝”强调可靠与可控，不强调神奇、全能或替代人。
- 英文不是逐字翻译；保证同一镜头时间内自然说完即可。
