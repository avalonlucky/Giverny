# Giverny · 36-Second Product Promo Script

## 简体中文（默认）

创作，不该散落在聊天、表格和网盘里。

Giverny 不只是任务管理。它把需求、排期、进展、工时、文件和验收，连成一条可追溯的工作链路。

团队一眼看清本月进度；每一次投入都有证据，每一份交付都跟着项目归档。

爱丽丝会读取真实业务上下文，先分析、再验真，写入前向你确认。

月底，结算回单一键生成，数据、文件和金额都能清楚分享。

Giverny，让可靠的工作留下证据，也让创作在自己的花园里生长。

## English

Creative work shouldn't be scattered across chats, spreadsheets, and drives.

Giverny is more than task management. It connects briefs, schedules, progress, time, files, and approval into one traceable workflow.

Your team sees the whole month at a glance. Every hour keeps its evidence, and every delivery stays with its project.

Alice reads real business context, analyzes first, verifies the facts, and asks before anything is written.

At month end, generate a settlement receipt in one click, ready to review and share.

Giverny makes reliable work traceable—and lets creation grow in its own garden.

## Pronunciation Notes

- Chinese: pronounce “Giverny” as the French place name, softly and without spelling letters individually.
- English: pronounce “Giverny” as “zhi-ver-nee”.
- “Alice” is the product agent name; the Chinese narration uses “爱丽丝”.
