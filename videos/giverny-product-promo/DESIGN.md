# Giverny Promo Design System

## Overview

Giverny uses a quiet editorial workspace built from warm paper surfaces, deep green-black text, hairline rules, and restrained sage accents. The product UI favors wide tables, slim status signals, precise numeric columns, and generous whitespace rather than decorative card grids. The brand layer introduces a softly moving water-lily garden, while the working layer stays direct and verifiable. The promo should preserve that tension: poetic atmosphere around rigorous operational evidence.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#FBFBF8` — warm paper canvas and UI background.
- **Primary Text**: `#1F2A27` — deep green-black for headlines and essential data.
- **Secondary Text**: `#586460` — muted copy, labels, and timestamps.
- **Muted Text**: `#66716D` — low-priority metadata.
- **Brand Sage**: `#3C6B61` — primary action, progress, and brand emphasis.
- **Brand Sage Strong**: `#2F564E` — dark progress bars and high-contrast accents.
- **Deep Menu Green**: `#20302C` — dark transition panels and final wordmark contrast.
- **Accepted Surface**: `#E9F5EA` — quiet completion/acceptance signal.
- **Alternate Surface**: `#F4F4EE` — task rows and secondary zones.
- **Warm Alert**: `#94652F` — sparing deadline/internal attention signal.

## Typography

- **Editorial voice**: Iowan Old Style (600) for the Giverny wordmark, emotional lines, and the final brand thought.
- **Product voice**: Inter (400, 500, 600, 700, 800) for interface labels, data, captions, and operational claims; this is the captured product font and is intentionally retained.
- **Hierarchy**: 88–132px display headlines, 30–44px supporting copy, 18–24px labels. Use tabular numerals for metrics.
- **Register**: serif communicates the garden and human intent; sans-serif communicates evidence and workflow.

## Elevation

Depth comes from layered paper planes, subtle `#1F2A27` hairlines, a localized sage glow, and shallow perspective on product screenshots. Product frames may use a soft floating shadow, but UI elements inside the screenshots must remain visually flat. Avoid glassmorphism and heavy black shadows.

## Components

- **Editorial App Shell**: left navigation rail, large top title, wide content canvas, and right-side detail pane.
- **Metric Ledger**: four large numeric columns separated by hairlines.
- **Task Flow Rows**: one strong task title, secondary metadata, and a compact right-side status/progress group.
- **Progress Detail Rail**: right-side timeline with percentage, segmented time entries, and evidence thumbnails.
- **Project File Archive**: month/project tree with verified delivery thumbnails.
- **Settlement Receipt**: wide branded table, summary metrics, and precise monetary totals.
- **Alice Agent Panel**: bright right-side assistant sheet that overlays the current business context.
- **Water-Lily Garden**: full-bleed atmospheric brand image with slow breeze and ripple motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep one visual focal point per shot and group status signals together.
- Use exact captured colors and preserve the UI's paper-and-ink hierarchy.
- Animate screenshots as physical planes with purposeful pan, focus, and perspective.
- Let garden motion feel organic while product motion feels precise and frame-driven.
- Use the logo in both the opening and closing beats.

### Don'ts

- Do not add neon, purple-blue gradients, glass cards, or generic SaaS glow.
- Do not cover product screenshots with so much copy that the UI becomes unreadable.
- Do not use decorative flowers, petals, or water-lily icons as filler.
- Do not invent new status colors or badge styles.
- Do not animate with random values, wall-clock time, CSS keyframes, or infinite loops.
